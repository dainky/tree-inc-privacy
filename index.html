Tree Inc

Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Tree Inc is a single-player tree-growing game. This policy explains the information used by the game, optional advertising and Apple services.

Publisher and contact

Publisher: Dainky Games
Privacy contact: dainkyrok@gmail.com

Your grove and settings

The game stores your trees, progress, inventory, rewards, purchase records and preferences on your device. These records make the game work, preserve purchases and prevent duplicate rewards. The game does not require a separate Tree Inc account. We do not operate a player database or a server that receives your grove.

On builds with configured iCloud backup, Apple may store a copy of your save in your own iCloud account. That copy includes the progress and reward records in the save. iCloud availability depends on your device, build and Apple account. Removing the local app does not necessarily remove an iCloud copy.

Optional advertisements

You can choose to watch a Google AdMob rewarded video for a temporary Leaf-production boost. The game does not automatically show advertisements. Playing the game does not require watching a video.

Google's advertising SDK may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate the service, prevent fraud, diagnose SDK problems, analyze usage and improve its services. This information may be associated with device or app identifiers even though Dainky Games does not operate a player database. Our release integration marks every ad request for child age treatment, limits ad content to Google's general-audience rating, disables ad personalization and the publisher first-party advertising identifier, and does not request or transmit Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). These settings apply to adults as well as children.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable privacy messages and choices. When the app starts, Google's privacy-messaging service may contact Google to refresh privacy information, even if you do not choose to watch a video. Ad initialization, loading and presentation require an explicit Watch action. We mark privacy-messaging requests as under the age of consent; it does not ask children to consent to personalized advertising. Where available, use Ad privacy choices in the game's video offer or Settings to review or change available choices. The integration does not request Apple's tracking permission. The game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising privacy information.

Apple purchases and services

Apple handles in-app checkout and payment information. The game receives verified purchase and entitlement information, including product and transaction identifiers, to deliver items, restore supported purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple account password.

Apple also provides iCloud and operating-system services where used. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's processing.

Reminders

Optional grove reminders use your saved game state and notification preferences to schedule local notifications on your device. The game has no remote push-notification server. You can turn reminders off in the game and manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Support messages

If you contact us, we use your address and the information you provide to respond and resolve your request. Send only the information needed for the issue. We keep support correspondence only as needed for support and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Storage and your choices

Local saves and purchase recovery records remain on your device until removed. Apple and Google manage retention of information held by their services under their own policies. Removing the game does not cancel purchases or necessarily delete information those services hold.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Contact the publisher using the privacy contact above. Use the in-game ad privacy controls for available advertising choices, and Apple account controls for Apple-managed information.

Children

Tree Inc is intended for children under 13 as well as teens and adults. We do not collect birth dates or create player profiles, and the game has no public chat or user-generated content. We apply the advertising restrictions described above to all players. Gameplay remains available without watching an ad or making a purchase. Parents can manage purchase permissions through Apple's family and device controls.

Parents or guardians can contact Dainky Games to ask about information provided directly to us, request its deletion or raise a concern. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending a support message. We do not use support messages to build advertising profiles.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
